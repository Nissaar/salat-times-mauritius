ℹ️ Information

Hanafi vs Shafi'i: The main difference is in Asr prayer timing. Hanafi calculates Asr when shadow is twice the object length, while Shafi'i calculates when shadow equals object length.

Forbidden Times: Prayer is forbidden during sunrise, solar noon (Istiwa/Zawaal), and sunset. These are marked with ⚠️.

Altitude Adjustment: Prayer times are adjusted based on your location's altitude for more accurate sunrise/sunset times.
